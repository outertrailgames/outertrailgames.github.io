HexGlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Draft — release review required

This policy describes the implementation reviewed on October 27, 2026. Before release, Outer Trail Games must confirm the final SDKs, advertising settings, audience declarations, and third-party retention and deletion details. This draft is not a claim that release compliance has been verified.

Outer Trail Games develops HexGlow, an Android puzzle game (application ID com.outertrailgames.hexglow). This policy explains how information is handled by the game and its integrated services.

Information collected and processed

HexGlow does not require an Outer Trail Games account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location to play. The reviewed game code does not send puzzle saves to a studio-operated server. These limits do not mean that the app’s third-party services collect no personal information.

The Android app uses internet access for advertising, consent, and Google Play purchase services. No separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting integration was identified in the reviewed code. Advertising services still process interaction and diagnostic data as described below.

The information handled by the app and its services includes:

  • Approximate location: inferred from an IP address by advertising services, rather than GPS location requested by the game.
  • App interactions: information such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics and other app performance data: information about the performance of the app and advertising SDK.
  • Device or other identifiers: advertising and app-set identifiers where available.
  • Age-group information: whether you selected under 16 or 16 or older, used for consent and advertising privacy settings.
  • Purchase history: information about your optional ad-free purchase, used to enable and restore ad-free access.

Advertising and third-party services

HexGlow integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for startup and between-puzzle advertisements. Google documents that its advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. These data are collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Diagnostic and performance information can include app launch time, hangs, and energy usage. Actual processing depends on the SDK version, device settings, and applicable privacy choices. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) handles consent messages and privacy choices. HexGlow requires you to choose under 16 or 16 or older and passes the resulting under-age-of-consent flag to Google’s consent and advertising SDKs. This shares age-group information with Google for advertising privacy settings and compliance; the choice is not kept solely on your device. The game does not ask for your exact age or date of birth. The game waits for UMP’s permission to request ads before loading them. Where Google requires a privacy options form, you can reopen it from Settings → Ad-free & privacy → Privacy choices. You can also change your age group in that panel. Available choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration.

You can use your Android device’s advertising privacy controls to reset or delete its advertising ID, where supported. These controls and declining personalized advertising do not necessarily stop all data processing, including IP-address processing by advertising or consent services. HexGlow does not provide a single switch that disables all Google service data collection.

Google Play purchases

The app integrates Google Play Billing for an optional permanent ad-free purchase and restoration of that purchase. Google handles checkout. HexGlow processes purchase status, product identifiers, and purchase tokens to recognize ownership, sends purchase tokens back to Google Play to acknowledge completed purchases, and stores an ownership flag locally. This information supports the ad-free feature and purchase restoration. Purchasing is optional. HexGlow does not send purchase records to a studio-operated server or supply them to its advertising SDK. The game does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Recognized ad-free ownership stops new ad requests and displays; it does not delete data previously processed by Google or stop purchase and consent services from operating.

Local device storage

HexGlow stores puzzle progress, attempts, generated puzzle definitions, and preferences such as appearance, audio, hints, and difficulty in its app storage. It keeps recovery copies of save data. It also stores your selected age group (under 16 or 16 or older), rather than your date of birth, and a cached ad-free ownership flag. Google’s SDKs may store their own service and consent information on your device.

The reviewed Android export disables Android app-data backup. HexGlow does not implement its own cloud-save service.

Children’s privacy

The recorded intended audience is teens and adults aged 13 and older; HexGlow is not intended for children under 13. Its age-group selection distinguishes under 16 from 16 or older and is used for advertising privacy settings. It is not age verification and does not separately identify children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through HexGlow, contact Outer Trail Games using the address below. The studio must confirm that its final Google Play audience declarations and advertising configuration match this intended audience before release.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until replaced or removed. To remove the app’s local data, clear HexGlow’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This erases local progress and preferences, including recovery saves and the cached purchase flag. A Google Play purchase can still be recognized through purchase restoration; clearing local data does not cancel or erase Google’s purchase records.

Data held by Google is subject to Google’s retention policies and controls. Clearing HexGlow’s storage does not delete information already transmitted to Google. See Google’s data retention explanation and Google’s data deletion guidance. Contact Outer Trail Games for questions about data handling or deletion. A provider-specific retention period has not been verified for this release.

Security

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local game data is stored in the app’s device storage; this policy does not promise that it is separately encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. The policy should be reviewed whenever the game’s services or data-handling practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Outer Trail Games at outertrailgames@gmail.com. Please identify HexGlow in your message and avoid sending payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
