HexGlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Outer Trail Games develops HexGlow, an Android puzzle game (application ID com.outertrailgames.hexglow). This policy explains how information is handled by the game and its integrated services.

Information collected and processed

HexGlow does not require an Outer Trail Games account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location to play. HexGlow does not send puzzle saves to a studio-operated server. These limits do not mean that the app’s third-party services collect no personal information.

The Android app uses internet access for advertising, consent, and Google Play purchase services. HexGlow does not use a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising services still process interaction and diagnostic data as described below.

The information handled by the app and its services includes:

  • Approximate location: inferred from an IP address by advertising services, rather than GPS location requested by the game.
  • App interactions: information such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics and other app performance data: information about the performance of the app and advertising SDK.
  • Device or other identifiers: app-set and other service identifiers, depending on the service and its settings. HexGlow configures ad requests to prevent transmission of the Android advertising ID, as described below.
  • Purchase history: information about your optional ad-free purchase, used to enable and restore ad-free access.

Advertising and third-party services

HexGlow integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for startup and between-puzzle advertisements. Google documents that, by default, its advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. These data are collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Diagnostic and performance information can include app launch time, hangs, and energy usage. Actual processing depends on the SDK version, device settings, and applicable privacy choices. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

HexGlow does not ask for your age or date of birth. Everyone receives the same ad settings: ads are limited to Google's Teen (T) content rating or lower, and Google's under-age-of-consent treatment is enabled for all ad requests. Google documents that this treatment disables personalized advertising and transmission of the Android advertising ID. HexGlow also disables Google's first-party advertising ID feature. These settings do not prevent all data processing, such as processing your IP address and ad interactions.

HexGlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether ads may be requested. The game waits for this check before loading ads. Because HexGlow enables under-age-of-consent treatment for everyone, UMP does not request consent from users. If Google reports that privacy options are available and required, a Privacy choices button appears under Settings > Ad-free & privacy. This button may not appear under the current settings. See Google's restricted ad-treatment guidance and UMP guidance.

Outside HexGlow, you can use your Android device’s advertising privacy controls to reset or delete its advertising ID, where supported. These system controls do not stop all data processing, including IP-address processing by advertising or consent services. HexGlow does not provide a single switch that disables all Google service data collection.

Google Play purchases

The app integrates Google Play Billing for an optional permanent ad-free purchase and restoration of that purchase. Google handles checkout. HexGlow processes purchase status, product identifiers, and purchase tokens to recognize ownership, sends purchase tokens back to Google Play to acknowledge completed purchases, and stores an ownership flag locally. This information supports the ad-free feature and purchase restoration. Purchasing is optional. HexGlow does not send purchase records to a studio-operated server or supply them to its advertising SDK. The game does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Recognized ad-free ownership stops new ad requests and displays; it does not delete data previously processed by Google or stop purchase and consent services from operating.

Local device storage

HexGlow stores puzzle progress, attempts, generated puzzle definitions, and preferences such as appearance, audio, hints, and difficulty in its app storage. It keeps recovery copies of save data. It also stores a cached ad-free ownership flag. Earlier versions may have saved an age choice locally; this version neither reads nor updates that unused file, which can be removed by clearing app storage. Google’s SDKs may store their own service and consent information on your device.

Android app-data backup is disabled for HexGlow, and the game does not sync your progress to the cloud.

Children’s privacy

HexGlow is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, not children under 13. It does not collect an age selection or independently verify age. The same teen-or-lower ad-content limit and conservative under-age-of-consent treatment apply to everyone.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through HexGlow, contact Outer Trail Games using the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until replaced or removed. To remove the app’s local data, clear HexGlow’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This erases local progress and preferences, including recovery saves and the cached purchase flag. A Google Play purchase can still be recognized through purchase restoration; clearing local data does not cancel or erase Google’s purchase records.

Data held by Google is subject to Google’s retention policies and controls. Clearing HexGlow’s storage does not delete information already transmitted to Google. See Google’s data retention explanation and Google’s data deletion guidance.

Outer Trail Games does not store your puzzle progress or purchase records on its own servers. To delete local game data, clear HexGlow’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. For information held by Google, use Google’s privacy and deletion controls linked above. For questions about this policy or information you send us by email, contact Outer Trail Games.

Security

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local game data is stored in the app’s device storage; HexGlow does not add its own encryption to local save files. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We update this policy when the game's services or information-handling practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Outer Trail Games at outertrailgames@gmail.com. Please identify HexGlow in your message and avoid sending payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
